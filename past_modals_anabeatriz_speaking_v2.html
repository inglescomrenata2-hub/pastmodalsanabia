Expressões-alvo

Deduzir: must've · might've · may've · can't have · couldn't have

Julgar / arrepender: should've · shouldn't have · could've (and didn't)

Imaginar: I would've… · I wouldn't have… · If I'd been there, I would've…

Pra ganhar tempo: Hmm, good question… · Let me think… · I mean… · Honestly? · To be fair…

Atividade pra fazer em voz alta, na aula. Use os campos só pra anotar palavras-chave, não precisa escrever frases completas. O timer serve pra você falar sem parar até o tempo acabar.

Parte 1 — Solve the mystery

Leia cada mini-história. Você tem 1 minuto pra levantar o máximo de hipóteses que conseguir sobre o que aconteceu, usando must've / might've / can't have. No final, diga qual é a sua aposta.

1. Your client Megan has never missed a Pilates class in two years. This week she didn't show up, didn't text, and her Instagram has been silent for five days.
Notas:

2. You come home and your front door is unlocked. Nothing is missing, but there's a bouquet of flowers on the kitchen table.
Notas:

3. Your neighbor, who always complains about noise, knocked on your door last night with a cake and a huge smile.
Notas:

4. Your Uber driver took a 40-minute route for a trip that usually takes 15, and kept looking in the rearview mirror.
Notas:

5. Your friend from Brazil posted a photo at the airport with the caption "Never again." 😤
Notas:

Exemplos de hipóteses:
1. She must've gotten sick. / She might've gone on a trip and forgotten to tell you. / She can't have quit — she loves the class!
2. My partner must've left them. / Someone might've had a spare key. / It can't have been a burglar — burglars don't bring flowers. 😅
3. He must've felt bad about complaining. / He might've won the lottery. / He must've been trying to make peace.
4. He must've been lost. / He might've been trying to avoid traffic. / He could've been trying to make more money — he can't have gotten lost for 40 minutes!
5. Her flight must've been cancelled. / She might've lost her luggage. / She must've had a terrible layover.

Parte 2 — What would you have done?

Leia as situações. Diga em voz alta: (a) o que a pessoa should've / shouldn't have feito e (b) o que você would've done no lugar dela. Fale por pelo menos 1 minuto em cada uma.

1. Carla moved to the US with her husband, quit her job in Brazil and didn't learn any English before the move. Now she feels lonely and stuck at home.
Notas:

2. A Pilates instructor noticed a client was doing an exercise wrong, but didn't say anything because she didn't want to be rude. The client hurt her back.
Notas:

3. Lucas spent all his savings on a road trip across the US. Now he's back home with no money, but says it was the best month of his life.
Notas:

4. Your friend took a year off work, but spent most of it scrolling on her phone and feeling guilty about it.
Notas:

Exemplos:
1. She should've taken some English classes before moving. She could've looked for a Brazilian community there. Honestly, I would've joined a gym or a class to meet people.
2. She should've corrected her — that's literally her job! She shouldn't have worried about being rude. I would've said something like "Let's try this a little differently."
3. He could've saved a little for when he got back… but to be fair, I would've done the same thing!
4. She should've made a plan. She shouldn't have been so hard on herself, though — rest is important too. I would've tried to learn something new.

Parte 3 — Role-play: "I'm so sorry!"

A professora faz o papel da outra pessoa. Você precisa pedir desculpas e explicar o que aconteceu, usando I should've… / I shouldn't have… / I could've… / I must've…. A outra pessoa vai reclamar um pouquinho, então mantenha a conversa!

Card A — The studio. You changed the time of your Saturday class but forgot to tell one of your clients. She drove 30 minutes and found the studio closed. She's calling you, not happy.
Notas:

Card B — The borrowed car. Your friend lent you her car for the weekend. You got a $75 parking ticket and only noticed it when you gave the car back.
Notas:

Card C — The birthday. You completely forgot your best friend's birthday. She saw you posting stories at the beach that same day.
Notas:

Frases úteis:
A: "Oh no, I'm so sorry! I should've texted everyone. I must've forgotten to add you to the group message. I could've at least called you. Let me give you a free class to make up for it."
B: "I'm so embarrassed. I shouldn't have parked there — I must've missed the sign. I should've checked before I gave the car back. Of course I'll pay for it."
C: "I'm the worst friend ever. I should've set a reminder. I can't believe I didn't call you — I must've lost track of the days. Let me take you out to dinner this weekend!"

Falas da professora pra puxar a conversa: "You could've at least texted me!" · "I would've never done that to you." · "You must've known!"

Parte 4 — Quick-fire round

Complete cada frase em voz alta, o mais rápido possível, e explique em 1 ou 2 frases. Tente terminar todas antes do tempo acabar!

  1. Last year, I really should've…
  2. When I first moved to the US, I couldn't have known that…
  3. I would've never become a physical therapist if…
  4. My worst purchase ever — I shouldn't have…
  5. The best decision I've made this year — I could've… but I didn't.
  6. Someone in my family must've been really surprised when…

Notas:

Respostas pessoais. Pontos pra professora observar:
✔ particípio correto depois de have (should've gone, could've taken, would've seen)
✔ pronúncia reduzida: should've /ˈʃʊdəv/, could've /ˈkʊdəv/, would've /ˈwʊdəv/ (nunca "should of")
✔ If I hadn't…, I would've… (e não "if I wouldn't have")
✔ fluência: ela usou conectores e preencheu as pausas em inglês?
